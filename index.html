Questions

Frequently Asked

Is fasting safe for women?

For many women, fasting can be a powerful tool when it's timed around hormonal shifts rather than applied the same way every day. It isn't right for everyone—for example during pregnancy, while breastfeeding, or with a history of disordered eating. Always check with your doctor before changing your eating patterns.

I don't have a regular cycle. Can I still work with you?

Yes. Whether your cycle is irregular, you're in perimenopause, or you're post-menopausal, we build a rhythm that supports your current biology.

Do I have to fast for long periods?

No. Fasting is a spectrum. We start where you are and build gradually, and some phases of your month call for little or no fasting at all.

Is coaching a replacement for medical care?

No. I'm a certified fasting coach, not a medical doctor. Coaching complements—never replaces—care from your healthcare provider.
